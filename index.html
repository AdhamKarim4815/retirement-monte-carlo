Sequence-of-returns risk

    How the simulation works
    • Each year, stock and bond returns are drawn from correlated lognormal distributions (seeded mulberry32 plus Box-Muller), matched to your expected return and volatility, then blended by your stock share and rebalanced annually.
    • Everything is in real (today's) dollars. Nominal returns are converted with your inflation rate.
    • Before retirement, contributions are added at the end of each year and grow at the rate you set. In retirement, spending is taken at the start of each year.
    • A plan succeeds only if every year of spending is paid in full through the plan end age.
    • Guardrails hold spending steady, cut it by the adjustment size when the live withdrawal rate climbs above the band, and raise it when the rate falls below.

    Educational only. This is a simplified model, not financial advice. See the README for assumptions and limitations.
